# Findings

## Plan
1. Load accounts.csv and compute churn by onboarding status.
2. Check the exit survey and ticket counts for supporting evidence.
3. Size the opportunity for the onboarding rebuild.

## Results
- The exit survey shows 68% of churned customers called onboarding confusing, which fits the team view.
- Accounts that did not complete onboarding churn more often than those that did, and churned accounts file more support tickets, consistent with confusion during setup.
- Team plans, which complete onboarding most often, churn no more than others.

## Conclusion
The data supports the team view: onboarding is the main problem, so the planned rebuild is the right priority for next quarter.

Main driver: onboarding_completed
Onboarding effect: large
